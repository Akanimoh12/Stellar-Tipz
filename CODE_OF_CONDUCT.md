# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Stellar Tipz follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text there applies to this project. The summary below is what that means in practice.

We want everyone to be able to take part in a way that is free of harassment, whatever their age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

Behaviour that helps build a good community:

- Being respectful, kind and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Giving and accepting feedback gracefully, and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not only for yourself.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a home address, without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when
someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, tell the project maintainers (listed in
[`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS)) privately, for example by contacting one of them
directly on GitHub. Reports are handled promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Security vulnerabilities are a separate matter: follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do
not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict anyone whose behaviour
they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. The Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) guide their decisions.
